How we decide which city opens next.

No paid shortcuts. No city opens because a partner lobbied for it. It opens because customers asked and we can serve them well.

  • Waitlist count. Enough demand means enough bookings in the first month. We don't launch into empty cities.
  • Partner availability. At least three verified companies per service, per city. Fewer than that, and customers can't compare.
  • Operational reach. Support has to cover the timezone and language without stretching the team thin.
