01 Step 1 of 7

You pick — we handle the rest

Open Cleanly and choose one of three services. Your city is detected from GPS; if you deny permission, pick it from a dropdown. Done in under 10 seconds.

  • GPS auto-detects your city across UAE, KSA, Egypt
  • Three service cards — car wash, sofa, carpet — with one-tap select
  • Browse anonymously — no signup to see prices
Privacy note: we don't ask for your phone number until you book. Browsing stays anonymous.

04 Step 4 of 7

Pay securely in-app

Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Samsung Pay — all through Stripe. Your payment is authorized but held until the service completes. If you never confirm, you never pay.

  • Cleanly handles payment, tax receipt, and refund
  • The company handles the clean itself, nothing else
  • No tipping screens — price is the price
PCI-safe: your card never touches our servers. Stripe handles it end-to-end.

07 Step 7 of 7

Confirm — or dispute

When the service ends, you get a "Confirm" notification. Tap confirm and leave a rating. Not happy? Tap dispute instead. You have 24 hours, and if we verify your complaint, the full payment is refunded.

  • Tap confirm → washer is paid, you rate 1–5 stars
  • Tap dispute → upload a photo, we review within 24 hrs
  • Verified dispute = full refund, no back-and-forth with the company
Why this matters: only when you tap Confirm is the washer actually paid. That's why they care about your rating.
