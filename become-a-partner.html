Before you apply

What you need to qualify.

Business

  • Trade license (DED, CR, or local equivalent)
  • Public liability insurance — minimum AED 250,000
  • Bank account in the country of operation
  • At least 1 trained washer on staff

Operations

  • Smartphone per washer (iOS 15+ or Android 10+)
  • Equipment meeting Cleanly's service checklist (steamer for sofa, vacuum + supplies for car, facility for carpet)
  • Response time: accept or reject jobs within 5 minutes during operating hours
  • Set and post operating hours so customers know when to book
Carpet-only note: carpet cleaning companies need a facility address registered with us — we don't approve carpet services via mobile-only setups.

Good to know

Partner FAQ

Can I set my own prices?

Yes, within category ranges that protect both sides. You can also offer promo rates in the dashboard — the customer sees your price, not ours.

What happens if a customer disputes a clean and I disagree?

We review the before/after photos, customer history, and your written response. Verified disputes are refunded from escrow; your payout is unaffected for valid services. Our rule: the photos decide, not who argues louder.

Do I have to accept every booking?

No. Accept or reject within 5 minutes. Rejection is neutral up to 15% of offers per week; beyond that it affects your ranking in the match list.

Can I pause the listing if I'm on holiday?

Yes — one-tap pause in the dashboard, up to 30 days per year. Your rating and rank are preserved; nothing resets.

What's the contract term?

Month-to-month. No lock-in, no cancellation penalty. Give us 14 days' notice and you're out — we'll finish outstanding jobs and pay out the last payout on schedule.
