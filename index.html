Good to know

Questions customers ask

What if I'm not satisfied with the service?

Open an in-app dispute within 24 hours. If the issue is verified, we refund you in full — you never pay for a clean you aren't happy with.

How are washers verified?

Every washer passes an ID check, submits insurance documents, and completes a 7-day trial period with supervised jobs before going live.

Is my payment safe?

We use Stripe with Apple Pay and Google Pay support. Your card details never touch our servers, and payment is held until the service completes.
