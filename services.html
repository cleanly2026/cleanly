Good to know

Service questions

Can I add a second car or second carpet to one booking?

Yes. On the booking screen, tap "Add another" before confirming. Each extra item has its own price and is handled as part of the same visit (car wash, sofa) or same pickup (carpet).

What cleaning products do you use? Are they pet-safe?

All approved companies use biodegradable, fragrance-light detergents that are safe around pets and children once dry. If you need a specific allergy-friendly product, add a note on booking and the company will confirm before arriving.

Do you offer subscription pricing for weekly car wash?

Yes. After your first car wash, you'll see a "Make this weekly" option in the receipt. Weekly plans run ~15% below the one-off price and you can skip or cancel a week anytime.

What if my carpet is damaged during cleaning?

Every pickup is covered by insurance from the moment the washer collects the carpet until it's returned to you. If anything goes wrong, open a dispute in-app and we'll handle the claim directly — you don't deal with the company.
