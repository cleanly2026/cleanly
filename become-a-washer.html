Before you apply

Washer FAQ

Can I work for other cleaning services at the same time?

Yes. Cleanly is non-exclusive. Work for us alongside any other platform or your own direct customers. We only ask that you respect the time windows you've accepted on Cleanly.

What if a customer cancels after I've arrived?

You're paid a trip fee automatically — it shows up in your weekly payout. If the customer cancels more than once, we pause their account while we investigate.

Who pays if I damage something during a job?

Cleanly's platform insurance covers accidental damage on verified jobs. Report it in-app immediately with photos; we handle the customer directly. Don't offer to pay out of pocket — that's not how this works.

Can I choose only certain types of jobs (e.g. car wash only)?

Yes. You select the services you want to do in your profile. Only matching jobs will show in your feed. You can change this anytime.

What happens if I get a bad rating I don't agree with?

You can request a review in-app within 48 hours. If your before/after photos show the job was done correctly, the rating is removed from your average. Ratings don't stick around when the evidence says otherwise.

How do I get my first job after joining?

Once your city opens to new washers, we'll notify you from the waitlist. Onboarding is a short video, a quiz, and a supervised first job. From sign-in to your first real job is usually within 3–5 days.
