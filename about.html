From the founders

Cleanly exists because we got tired of calling three car wash shops and getting three different prices — none of them written down, all of them negotiable once the washer showed up. Getting a trusted cleaning service in the Gulf was WhatsApp-roulette: you sent the address, they sent a number, you hoped for the best.

We decided a few things early and haven't changed our minds. Car wash and sofa cleaning happen where you are — no moving your car, no renting a wet-vac. Carpets get picked up, cleaned at a facility, returned on your schedule — because nobody has space to deep-clean a 3-meter rug in their apartment. Every price is shown before you book. And the washer doesn't get paid until you say the job is done.

There's a list of things we refuse to do. No tipping screens at checkout. No surge pricing at 3am. No commission above 15%. No hiding behind "final price quoted on arrival." It bothered us that "insurance" sometimes meant the company owner promising you something over WhatsApp. So every partner on Cleanly carries public liability coverage we verify ourselves.

We pay attention to Gulf details because we live them. Early-morning car washes in July before the sun makes the paint untouchable. Booking windows that respect Ramadan schedules. Compound gate codes in the notes field. Arabic from day one — not retrofitted, not machine-translated. If you notice the small things, it's because we did.

— The Cleanly team · Dubai · Riyadh · Cairo

What we believe

What we believe about cleaning as a service.

Price transparency is non-negotiable

If you can't see the price before you book, you're the product.

Trust is earned per-job

One bad clean breaks 50 good ones. We over-index on verification.

Cash is the enemy

Stripe holds money so the washer gets paid and you get refunded if needed. No one argues in the lobby.

The Gulf is the point

We don't copy Deliveroo's playbook. Ramadan timing, compound access, bilingual from day one.

Honest distinctions

Things Cleanly is not.

Not a tipping economy

Price is the price. No "if you liked the service…" pressure screens at checkout.

Not a surge-pricing platform

A 3am booking costs the same as a 3pm booking. Your price doesn't move with demand.

Not the cheapest option

We prioritize verified over cheap. Unverified cleaning has its own marketplaces; they're not us.

Not a gig app

We partner with registered companies, not individual contractors. A dedicated washer app is coming Q3.
