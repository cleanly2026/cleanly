Refunds

When we refund, and how long it takes.

Every refund scenario below is written from the app's actual policy, not a generic template. Payment is held at Stripe, so when a refund is triggered, the money comes back from escrow — not from us chasing the partner company for it.

Washer didn't arrive

Full refund, automatic. You don't need to open a ticket — the app knows they never started the job.

Turnaround: within 24 hours

Service was incomplete

Open a dispute in-app with a photo showing the issue. If verified against the washer's before/after photos, we refund you. If evidence is mixed, we coach the company and give you credit.

Turnaround: 48 hours

Damage to your property

Not a refund — an insurance claim. See the Insurance section. Photos required within 24 hours of job completion.

See: Insurance

You canceled late, but we hadn't dispatched

Full refund. The washer never left, so there's no trip to compensate.

Turnaround: immediate

Carpet returned late

24+ hr late: 25% auto-refund. 48+ hr late: 50%. 72+ hr late: full refund — and the carpet is still returned.

Turnaround: auto-applied on return

How to request a refund

  1. Open the order in the Cleanly app.
  2. Tap Report issue. Select the scenario that fits.
  3. Upload photos if the app asks. Submit.

No phone number required. No hold music. That's intentional.

Insurance

What's covered, and what isn't.

Cleanly's platform insurance is supplemental to each partner company's own public liability policy. If something goes wrong, we route the claim — you don't deal with the company or the insurer directly.

Covered
  • Accidental damage to your property during a service
  • Damage to carpets during pickup, transport, or cleaning
  • Washer injury on your property (no liability on you)
  • Loss of carpet while in our facility's custody
Not covered
  • Pre-existing damage (before the washer arrived)
  • Items marked "fragile" that weren't disclosed at booking
  • Property owned by someone else (landlord's shared furniture, etc.)
  • Valuables over AED 5,000 not declared before the job
Coverage cap per incident AED 10,000 Applies to a single job. Items declared at booking can have individual higher limits.
Annual cap per customer AED 50,000 Across all jobs in a calendar year. Reset every January.

Claims process: Take photos immediately. Open the dispute in-app. We handle the claim with the insurer — you don't talk to the partner company, you don't talk to the insurer. Expect a decision within 5 business days.

Disputes

How we decide who's right.

Disputes are resolved on evidence, not on volume. The photos the washer uploaded + whatever you can upload are the record. We read both, apply the rules below, and decide in 24 hours.

What happened Evidence needed Outcome
Service quality dispute Before/after photos + your detail in the ticket Partial or full refund if photos support you
Damage claim Before/after photos + damage photos + repair quote Insurance claim routed (see Insurance section)
Washer no-show Automatic — the app knows when they didn't start Full refund + booking credit for the inconvenience
Cancellation mid-job App log shows when and why it ended Refund proportional to work completed (20% minimum floor)
One honest note: repeat bad-faith disputes — the kind where photo evidence clearly contradicts the claim — can lead to booking restrictions on your account. We don't talk about this much, but it's real. Our washers' livelihoods depend on the system working honestly for everyone.

Cancellation

When you can cancel, and what it costs.

Service When Fee
Car wash / Sofa More than 2 hrs before arrival Free
Less than 2 hrs before arrival 50% fee
After washer is dispatched 100% fee
Carpet Before pickup Free
During pickup window 30% fee
After pickup (carpet at facility) 50% refund if you refuse return

Why the fees? The fee covers the washer's lost time — it's paid to the washer, not to Cleanly. The closer to the job you cancel, the more of their day was committed.

How to cancel

  1. Open the order in the Cleanly app.
  2. Tap Cancel.
  3. Confirm the fee shown (if any). Done.

You never need to call. If the fee seems wrong, open a dispute — we'll read the logs.

Privacy

What we collect, what we don't.

Short version: we collect what the job needs. We don't collect what the job doesn't. We never sell data. The full legal detail is in the Privacy Policy (link below).

We collect
  • Your phone number (encrypted at rest; masked to washers)
  • Service address per booking (not shared beyond the assigned washer)
  • Payment method (held by Stripe, not us)
  • Job ratings and complaint history (to run the marketplace fairly)
We don't collect
  • Your location when you're not actively booking
  • Your contacts
  • Photos from your phone gallery (beyond what you upload)
  • Browsing data from other sites or apps

What the washer sees: your first name, service address, and job notes — only during the job window. Afterwards only the last 4 digits of your address and your rating remain visible to them. They never see your full phone number.

For legal detail, see the full Privacy Policy.

Data export or deletion requests (GDPR, PDPL, UAE federal data protection): email privacy@cleanly.ae. We reply within 30 days.
